## 1. Problem statement

| | |
|---|---|
| **The problem of** | walk-in-only access, with a fixed number of cards handed out at the gate each morning |
| **affects** | the patients of Midlands Community Clinic's Scottsville, Edendale and Imbali branches, most of all older people, parents with young children and people with disabilities, as well as the clinic staff who manage the morning rush |
| **the impact of which is** | pre-dawn queues, an average wait of 4 hours 12 minutes, about 40 people a day sent home without care, 22% of follow-up visits missed, and no data to plan services |
| **a successful solution would** | let patients reserve a time before leaving home through a channel that works for them, give staff a list of expected patients each day, remind patients of their visits, and protect space for walk-ins and emergencies |

## 2. Goals and KPIs

| Goal | KPI | Baseline (Nov 2026) | Target | How it will be measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Patients wait less | Average arrival-to-consultation time for booked patients | 4 h 12 min | Under 90 min | Check-in and consultation timestamps |
| Fewer wasted trips | Patients turned away per day (3 branches) | About 40 | Under 8 | Daily turned-away count at reception |
| Fewer missed visits | Follow-up no-show rate | 22% | 10% or less | Bookings marked "not arrived" |
| Planned access | Share of visits booked in advance | 0% | At least 40% | Bookings ÷ total visits |
| Faster mornings | Average folder retrieval time at the counter | 6 min | Under 1 min | Spot checks by sisters-in-charge |
| Fair access | Booking screens meeting WCAG 2.2 AA | Not applicable | 100% | Accessibility audit and screen-reader testing |
| Walk-ins protected | Share of daily capacity kept for walk-ins | Not applicable | At least 30% | Capacity settings per branch |
